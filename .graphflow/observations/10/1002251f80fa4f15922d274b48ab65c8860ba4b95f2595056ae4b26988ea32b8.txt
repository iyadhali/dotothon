Criterion Weight What judges look for
Technical execution 20% Live demo works; you explain method, results, limits
Data utilization 15% Data utilization measures how well a team used the data to
answer its question. Teams may use the datasets provided by the
organisers, publicly available data, or both.
Impact 15% A clear user and a clear action, backed by evidence
Scalability and readiness 15% Named user, maintainable tools, realistic roadmap
Relevance to the theme 10% A specific transport problem rooted in the Maldives
Innovation 10% An original question or method, beyond standard charts
Teamwork 10% Everyone contributes; several members speak
Clarity of presentation 5% Clear story, readable visuals, on time, direct answers
