What the ranking does

one facility at a time, on the same network the map uses

For every island that has no {{ stype_label|lower }}, we ask how many people who are currently more than two hours away would fall inside two hours if one were built there. The islands are then ranked and the best pick is added to the network before the next round, so the second and third picks account for the first. It is a greedy ranking on observed connectivity: it knows population and travel time, and nothing about land, staffing or cost.
