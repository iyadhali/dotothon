# Presentation Outline

## 1. Title Slide
- Team Name
- Project Title: Multi-Modal Accessibility to Key Services in the Maldives
- Mode of Transport: Multi-Modal (Land, Sea, Air)
- Datathon 2026

## 2. Problem Statement
- Uneven access to key services (universities, schools) across the Maldives archipelago due to geographical fragmentation
- Many islands lack feasible transport links — long travel times, multiple transfers, unreliable schedules
- Equity concern: where access is not feasible, service provision and transport planning must compensate

## 3. Research & Context
- Archipelagic nature of Maldives - ~200 inhabited islands across 26 atolls
- Services concentrated in Male' and regional hubs
- Reliance on sea and air transport; limited land connectivity
- Reference to HeiGIT/OpenAccessLens accessibility research

## 4. Objectives
- Display accessibility (travel distance/time) to key services (universities, schools) considering land, sea, and air transport
- Assess access for populations living on various islands
- Visualize results in QGIS to identify underserved areas

## 5. Data & Datasets Used
- MACL (Airport Traffic) - Air connectivity
- MTCC (Ferry/Bus data) - Sea and land transport in Greater Male'
- FollowMe/Avas Ride - Land transport/ride-hailing
- Island boundaries, population data, POIs (schools/universities)
- Team-shared datasets

## 6. Methodology
- Built multi-modal transport network (land + sea + air)
- Identified locations of universities and schools
- Calculated shortest travel time/distance from each inhabited island to nearest key service
- Accounted for transfers between modes
- Classified islands by accessibility levels
- Mapped results in QGIS

## 7. Results & Findings
- Map showing accessibility to universities/schools across islands
- Identification of underserved islands/atolls with limited access
- Population impact analysis
- Mode analysis - which transport combinations serve different islands

## 8. Conclusion
- Highlight geographic disparities in access to education/services
- Demonstrate value of multi-modal analysis for transport planning
- Results can inform policy to improve equitable access

## 9. Recommendations & Future Work
- Include more service types (healthcare) and transport modes
- Incorporate schedules/frequency for more realistic accessibility
- Consider cost/accessibility tradeoffs
- Expand to full network with travel costs
