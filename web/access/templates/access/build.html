Where should we build connectivity?

one new crossing, measured against the same network

A crossing that does not exist in the GPS month, between two islands no more than 60 km apart, sailed at 20 knots plus half an hour to connect — the same assumption the findings note uses. For each pair the page re-derives every island's travel time with that one edge added, then counts the residents who come within two hours, and the residents whose trip gets at least half an hour shorter.

Method. Travel time comes from the same graph the map uses: median observed crossing times, a one hour connection penalty when a journey changes boat, at most five sailings. The census is the 2022 count; children are 0–17. No construction cost, land, staffing or demand is modelled — this ranks one question on the network we can see, nothing else.
