Method, and what it does not claim

Trips. A stay is five or more consecutive GPS polls under two knots; a trip is the leg between two stays. The edge weight is the median observed crossing time for that pair of islands, and the count of trips is published beside it.

Journeys. One hour is added for each connection and a journey may use at most two sailings, which is what makes Addu's internal hops cheap and the capital reachable.

Roads. Addu's six census islands are joined by causeway because the boat data cannot see them. No other land link is assumed.

Limits. One month of movement data, not a timetable: a route with no observation is unknown, not closed. Air is not yet in the network, so Addu and Fuvahmulah show no sea link to the capital. Population is resident population, which includes foreign residents.
