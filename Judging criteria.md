
Criterion Weight What judges look for
Relevance to the theme 10.00% Does your project tackle a real transport and mobility question for the Maldives?
Data Utilization 15.00% Is the data right for your question, used correctly and properly sourced? Only organiser-provided datasets, self-collected data which are shared with organisers and publicly open data are allowed.
Innovation 10.00% Is your question, method or product original, beyond a standard set of charts? A platform, app or dashboard is not innovative on its own: judges look at what is new in the idea, the method or the way it is used.
Technical execution 20.00% Does your demo work live? Can you explain how it works and why you chose it? Do your results hold up, with limits named?
Impact 15.00% Would your findings help real decision-makers? Who benefits and what could they do differently?
Scalability and implementation readiness 15.00% Can it be built out after the datathon? Does it work with updated data, on tools others can maintain, for a named user, with a realistic plan?
Clarity of presentation 5.00% Clear story, readable visuals, on time, direct answers in Q&A
Teamwork 10.00% Did the whole team contribute? Clear roles, several members speaking, collaboration visible
